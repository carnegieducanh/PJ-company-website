Parts Supply

Pegatron group's contract manufacturing sites has the capacity to produce 2 million motherboards and 100,000 notebook PCs per month for major global PC and smartphone brands.

Semiconductor ICs and electronic components essential for the production of these 3Cs devices can be procured through global supply chains mainly in China and other regions of the world.

Pegatron Japan will supply necessary parts to customers in Japan by taking advantage of our large buying power through the Pegatron Group's global supply chains mainly from mainland China.
